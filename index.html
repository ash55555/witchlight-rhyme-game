How to play

Everyone floats up from the teapot in a bubble. A word appears and you take turns rhyming with it. Every real rhyme lifts your bubble higher. If your word is real but does not rhyme, or time runs out, your bubble pops and you fall. The last bubble in the sky wins.

Rhymes can be more than one word (for example, toilet and boil it). Every word must be a real English word. You cannot repeat a word, and you cannot use a word that sounds exactly like one already said.
